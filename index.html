The Puzzle Page — Support

A detective game set in 1924 New York, solved through word and logic puzzles.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Tap Help on any puzzle: Mr. Pim of the archive gives two nudges and then, if you ask twice, the answer. It is free, as often as you like.

Purchases

Bought a Season Pass or a case on another device, or after reinstalling? Open the season page from any locked case and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
