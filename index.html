Iris Vale: Crossword Detective — Support

A detective game set in 1924 New York, solved through word and logic puzzles.

Stuck on a puzzle?

Tap Help on any puzzle. Mr. Pim of the archive gives you two nudges for free. If you are still stuck, he can fill in a letter, a word or the whole answer for favors, which you earn by solving puzzles. Hints never cost money.

Purchases

Bought a Season Pass or a case on another device, or after reinstalling? Open Settings (the cog on the front page) and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
